# Announcement Templates

For `#announcements` (human mods only — never bots/webhooks). Every
announcement ends with a question: it reliably drives replies in `#general`.

---

## Weekly event — Friday Game Night (first announcement)

> ## 🎮 NEW: Friday Game Night — every week!
>
> Starting this Friday at **[TIME + timezone]**, we're running Origins arena battles in voice. All ranks welcome — hop into **Lobby**, we'll split into Game Rooms from there.
>
> 📅 RSVP in the Events calendar so you get a reminder.
>
> What's your ladder team this season? Drop it in #general 👇

## Weekly event — recurring reminder (Carl-bot scheduled, 2h before)

> 🎮 **Friday Arena Night in 2 hours!** Hop into the Lobby voice channel. All ranks welcome.
