These are wallet drainers. If a real drop happens, it will be in **#announcements** — posted by a human mod, nowhere else.

No OTC deals, even with people who seem trustworthy. We cannot help you recover anything lost in a private trade.

Click the server name → *Privacy Settings* → toggle **off** *Direct Messages*. Done — you've blocked the #1 attack.
