## 📜 Server Rules

**1. Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks. Banter is fine; punching down isn't.

**2. No scams, phishing, or impersonation.** Instant ban. This includes fake "support", fake giveaways, and wallet-drainer links.

**3. Mods will NEVER DM you first.** Anyone DMing you about "support tickets", "claims", or "verification" is a scammer. Report them in #scam-alerts threads.

**4. No unsolicited DMs and no OTC deals.** All trading talk stays in #marketplace-talk. Use the official marketplace — we can't help you recover an OTC scam.

**5. Keep channels on topic.** Memes in #memes, trading in #marketplace-talk, everything else in #general.

**6. No advertising or self-promo without mod approval.** This includes other servers, referral links, and token shills.

**7. English in public channels** (or the server's main language) so mods can moderate.

**8. Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.**

Breaking rules gets you a warning → timeout → ban, at mod discretion. Scams and drainer links skip straight to ban.

*Questions about a rule? Ask a mod in #general — don't DM.*
