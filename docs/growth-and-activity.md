# Growth & Member Activity

At 100–500 members, **retention beats acquisition** — a busy server grows
itself. Engagement work comes first; growth work rides on top of it.

## Engagement — keep the members you have

### Weekly rhythm (the core habit-builder)
Pick 1–2 recurring anchors and never skip them. Consistency beats novelty.

| Anchor | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Friday Game Night** | Fridays, evening | Origins arena battles in voice (`Game Room 1/2`), casual, open to all |
| **Weekly team-comp challenge** | Posted Mondays | A constraint prompt in the `#guides-and-resources` forum (e.g. "best team under X budget", "no plant class") — winner featured in the weekly shoutout |

Both go in the **Discord Events calendar** so members get notified and can
RSVP. Announcement templates:
[`../copy-paste/announcement-templates.md`](../copy-paste/announcement-templates.md).

### Seasonal leaderboards
- Each Origins season: post a server leaderboard of members' ranked climbs
  (collect via a pinned thread in `#origins`).
- Top 3 each season get a special role (e.g. `Season Champion 🏆`) until the
  next season ends.

### Celebrate people
- Once a week, pull the best post from `#clips-and-wins` into an
  `#announcements` shoutout. Costs nothing, makes members feel seen.
- **End every announcement with a question** ("What's your ladder team this
  season?") — it reliably drives replies in `#general`.

## Growth — bring new people in

### 1. Ride the Atia's Legacy wave (best 2026 hook)
The Axie MMO is in pre-registration. Position the server as **the place to
prep for launch**: theorycrafting in `#atias-legacy`, guild/party planning,
news the moment it drops. Mention it in every outside post.

### 2. Post where Axie players already are
- Official Axie Discord community spaces
- r/AxieInfinity
- X/Twitter threads during **season starts and patch days** (highest attention)

Share genuinely useful content — a guide, a tier list, a team-comp
spreadsheet — with a soft invite ("we theorycraft this stuff weekly in our
server"), never bare invite links.

### 3. Invite competition
Invite Tracker + a monthly reward role (e.g. `Recruiter of the Month`) for
top inviters. Works well at this size; keep rewards cosmetic to avoid
farming.

### 4. Partnerships
Find 2–3 similar-size Axie / web3 gaming servers and do event swaps: joint
tournaments, shared AMAs on the `Events Stage`. Both sides grow; neither
pays.

### 5. Server Discovery at 500
Enable Server Discovery once past 500 members — another reason to enable
Community now and keep Insights clean.

## Measure it (monthly, 15 minutes)

Server Insights (free with Community) — check on the 1st of each month:

1. **New-member retention** — are joiners still active after a week? If
   not, look at onboarding and whether `#introductions` posts get replies.
2. **Channel activity** — any channel quiet for a week gets merged or
   archived (see the consolidation pass in
   [`channel-structure.md`](channel-structure.md)).
3. **Event attendance** — RSVP + actual voice attendance for game night.
   If it's declining, change the time before changing the event.
